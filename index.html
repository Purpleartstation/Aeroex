What happens after your CNC door opens? The air quality challenge shouldn’t end at the machine.

You invested in a high-end CNC machine. You have air filtration pulling mist, smoke, and particulates out of the enclosure. Inside that cabinet, air quality is handled. But the moment that door opens — the challenge doesn’t end. It moves.

Wet parts come out coated in coolant, oil, and metalworking fluid. And right there, at the workbench, your machinist does what machinists have always done — manually blow-off, wipe down, and clean by hand. That high-pressure blast shears fluid into a fine aerosol mist, launched directly into your operator’s breathing zone. Sharp swarf scatters across the floor. Oil settles on nearby equipment. The overhead fan isn’t containing it. It’s just spreading it across the shop — shift after shift, day after day.

Aeroex closes that loop — across every stage of your process. At IMTS 2026, we are bringing our full lineup of engineered clean air solutions — from industrial mist collectors and ambient air filtration systems to point-of-use source-containment stations.

One of the systems on display is the BlowBox AX-1200 — a fully enclosed automated part cleaning and drying system designed to be installed alongside CNC machines or integrated into an automated manufacturing cell.

New BlowBox automation units can be customized and configured to fit your exact application, workflow and workshop requirements. Stop by Booth #135684 at McCormick Place and see the full Aeroex lineup live.
